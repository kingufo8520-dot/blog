1. Anguk Station
"We are starting our tour at Anguk Station. This area is the gateway to Seoul's most historical neighborhoods, connecting modern city life with traditional Korean palaces and Hanok villages."

2. Songhyeon Green Plaza
"This is Songhyeon Green Plaza. It was closed to the public for over a century due to historical reasons, but it recently opened as a peaceful park. It’s a great spot to enjoy a relaxing walk right in the middle of the city."

3. National Museum of Korean Contemporary History
"This is the National Museum of Korean Contemporary History. It shows how Korea transformed rapidly over the last century. Also, there is a rooftop garden here where we can get one of the best panoramic views of Gyeongbokgung Palace and the mountains behind it."

4. OIMU Bukchon
"OIMU is a local design studio that gives traditional Korean elements a modern twist. Their main products include beautifully designed traditional incense sticks, retro-style matches, and modern prints of Korean folk paintings (Minhwa). It’s a perfect place to see highly refined Korean crafts and pick up some unique souvenirs."

5. Magpie & Tiger Bukchon Tea Store
"Let’s take a break here. This is a modern tea house focusing on East Asian teas. Their main menu features a variety of traditional loose-leaf teas like Hadong green tea and fermented teas, beautifully paired with delicate Korean seasonal desserts. We can experience a very calm and serene tea culture and rest our legs before dinner."

6. Hoesik Jongno Anguk
"For our final stop, we are having dinner at 'Hoesik'. It’s a cozy local restaurant where we can enjoy a warm and hearty meal to wrap up our day. Their signature dishes include neat and flavorful modern Korean-Asian comfort foods, such as savory rice bowls and warm dishes prepared with fresh ingredients."
